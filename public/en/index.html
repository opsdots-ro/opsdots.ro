Services

Deep expertise where it matters: data, containers, systems.

Practical engineering, not reports that gather dust. We work directly on the systems your business depends on — and stay until they run flawlessly.

Database administration (DBA)

Dedicated or on-demand DBA for production databases: performance, data safety and availability.

  • Query, index and configuration tuning
  • Replication and HA clusters (Patroni, Percona XtraDB Cluster, Galera, MySQL Group Replication)
  • Backup (pgBackRest, Percona XtraBackup), point-in-time recovery and regular restore tests
  • Version upgrades and migrations (MySQL → Percona / PostgreSQL)
postgresql · mysql · percona · mariadb · mongodb · redis

Kubernetes platforms

We build and run Kubernetes on-premises, in the cloud or hybrid — GitOps by default, upgrades handled as engineering work.

  • Production cluster design and rollout
  • GitOps with FluxCD or ArgoCD, CI/CD and delivery pipelines
  • Security: RBAC, policy-as-code, secrets, signed images
  • Observability: metrics, logs, alerting
kubernetes · openshift · rancher · eks · aks · fluxcd · argocd · helm · prometheus

Linux server administration

Stable, secure, up-to-date servers — automated with Ansible, continuously monitored, fully documented.

  • CIS hardening and patch management
  • Automation with Ansible and Terraform
  • Virtualisation: VMware, Proxmox, KVM
  • Performance and incident troubleshooting
rhel · rocky · ubuntu · debian · ansible · terraform · systemd

Security & compliance

FAQ

What clients ask us.

What services does OpsDots offer?

Database administration (DBA), design and operation of Kubernetes platforms and Linux server administration — delivered as projects, fixed-price audits, team extension or 24/7 managed services.

Which databases do you manage?

PostgreSQL, MySQL, Percona Server for MySQL, MariaDB, MongoDB and Redis — including replication, high-availability clusters (Patroni, Percona XtraDB Cluster, Galera, MySQL Group Replication), backup and disaster recovery.

Do you work with on-premises Kubernetes or only in the cloud?

Both. We build and run clusters on-premises (bare metal, VMware, Proxmox), in the cloud (EKS, AKS, GKE) and hybrid, including air-gapped environments.

Do you provide 24/7 support?

Yes. With managed services, systems are monitored around the clock and critical incidents are handled by an on-call engineer under the agreed SLA.

Where do you operate?

We are based in Romania and work with clients across Romania and the European Union, remotely or on site.

How does an engagement start?

With a free 30-minute technical call, followed by a proposal: a fixed-scope audit, a project with clear deliverables or a managed services contract.
